COMANDO WEBINAR: BEAT BOARD VISUAL SCRIPT

Style anchor (keep consistent across every beat): clean Apple-like minimalism, soft diffused studio light, frosted-glass surfaces, off-white background (#f5f5f7), single blue accent (#0071e3), SF-Pro-like typography, calm and premium, no garish colors, no clutter, 16:9.

Beat 1: Fragmented chaos
Visual Description: Wide shot of a focused professional sitting at a clean desk, surrounded by a dozen translucent floating app windows that overlap and crowd the frame — a to-do list, a calendar grid, a spreadsheet, a paper notebook, chat bubbles, red notification badges. The windows tilt at clashing angles and partly obscure the person's face, conveying overload and divided attention. Cool desaturated tones, slight depth-of-field blur on the farthest windows. Composition feels busy and off-balance on purpose, person centered but visually buried under the interface noise.
Lighting & Mood: Flat cool daylight with weak shadows; faintly anxious, scattered mood. Notification badges glow a muted red as the only warm accents amid grey-blue panels.

Beat 2: Convergence into Comando
Visual Description: The same scattered windows lose their color, slow down, and stream inward like soft particles toward the center of the frame, dissolving into a single rounded frosted-glass panel. As they merge, the clutter disappears and the Comando logo resolves crisply at the center on the calm off-white field. The professional's shoulders relax; the frame becomes symmetrical and quiet. Negative space opens up around the single glass surface, signaling order replacing chaos.
Lighting & Mood: Light warms and softens; a gentle blue rim-glow traces the glass panel. Serene, premium, relief — the visual exhale after Beat 1.

Beat 3: The map of eight areas
Visual Description: Centered Comando logo on frosted glass, with eight simple line icons arranged in a slow elegant orbit around it — tasks, calendar, payments, projects, goals, notes, companies, and a heart-pulse for life. Thin blue connecting arcs link each icon to the center. Clean isometric-ish depth, generous spacing, each icon labeled in small understated type. Feels like an interface diagram rendered as a calm product hero shot.
Lighting & Mood: Even soft key light, subtle ambient occlusion under the glass; confident and explanatory. Blue accents on arcs and active icon; everything else neutral grey.

Beat 4: Real-time bridge (delegation)
Visual Description: Two stylized device screens face each other across the frame on a clean surface. A single task card detaches from the left "my" screen and glides along a thin luminous blue thread to the right "Maria" screen, where it lands and settles instantly. A small "→ Maria" tag lights up on the card. Motion-implied with a soft trailing streak; both screens show the same minimalist task UI to reinforce they are in sync.
Lighting & Mood: Soft studio light, the blue thread is the brightest element, casting a faint glow on both screens. Crisp, satisfying, "instant" feeling; calm confidence.

Beat 5: Offline to synced
Visual Description: A close, clean shot of the same task card being edited while a small "no connection" icon pulses discreetly in the corner; the card remains fully responsive. Then a single blue pulse ripples outward across the interface as a wifi/sync icon reconnects, and a subtle checkmark settles on the card. Background stays the calm off-white field; everything reads as effortless and reassuring.
Lighting & Mood: Soft and steady; the offline icon is a muted amber, the reconnect pulse a clean blue sweep. Mood: dependable, frictionless, quietly triumphant.

Beat 6: Close / CTA
Visual Description: Full-frame hero: the Comando logo centered on a large frosted-glass surface over the off-white field. Below it, the line "Trabalho e vida, no mesmo comando." fades in, in clean understated type, followed by a softly glowing "Comece agora" button. Lots of negative space, perfectly symmetrical, premium and final. A faint blue light blooms behind the logo.
Lighting & Mood: Warm-neutral key with a gentle blue backlight bloom; aspirational, calm, conclusive. The CTA button is the single brightest, most saturated element.
