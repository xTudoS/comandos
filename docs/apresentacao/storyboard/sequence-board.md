# Sequence Board — Webinar Comando

Cada sequência tem 4 panels contínuos e herda a identidade visual do beat board (Apple-like minimal, vidro fosco, fundo off-white #f5f5f7, acento azul #0071e3, luz suave, 16:9). Mantém eixo estável entre panels.

## Sequence A: Abertura (Beats 1 → 2)

### Panel 1
Wide shot. Focused professional at a clean desk, a dozen translucent app windows overlapping at clashing angles around them — todo list, calendar, spreadsheet, notebook, red notification badges — cool desaturated tones, busy off-balance frame, person partly buried under interface noise.

### Panel 2
Same wide shot, slight push-in. The clutter peaks — one extra notification badge pops — then the windows begin to lose color and tilt toward the center, motion just starting; the person's expression shifts from strained to attentive.

### Panel 3
Medium shot. The colorless windows stream inward as soft particles, converging toward a forming rounded frosted-glass panel at center; the desk and background simplify and quiet down; a faint blue rim-glow appears on the emerging glass.

### Panel 4
Centered hero. All clutter is gone; the Comando logo resolves crisply on the single frosted-glass panel over a calm off-white field, generous negative space, gentle blue glow, the professional's shoulders relaxed — order replacing chaos.

## Sequence B: Ponte do tempo real (Beat 4)

### Panel 1
Two minimalist device screens face each other on a clean surface, both showing the same task UI; on the left "my" screen a single task card is highlighted, soft studio light, calm off-white field.

### Panel 2
Close on the left screen as the task card detaches and lifts slightly, a thin luminous blue thread beginning to extend from it toward the right; soft trailing streak implies motion.

### Panel 3
Wide of both screens, the card mid-glide along the bright blue thread across the gap between screens — the thread is the brightest element, casting a faint glow on both devices.

### Panel 4
Close on the right "Maria" screen as the card lands and settles into place; a small "→ Maria" tag lights up; subtle settle animation implied, crisp and satisfying "instant sync" feeling.

## Sequence C: Encerramento / CTA (Beat 6)

### Panel 1
Full frame, mostly empty off-white field with a large frosted-glass surface fading in at center, faint blue backlight beginning to bloom behind it.

### Panel 2
The Comando logo resolves crisply at the center of the glass surface, perfectly symmetrical, premium and calm, blue bloom intensifying gently behind it.

### Panel 3
The tagline "Trabalho e vida, no mesmo comando." fades in below the logo in clean understated type, ample negative space preserved.

### Panel 4
A softly glowing "Comece agora" button fades in beneath the tagline as the single brightest, most saturated element; warm-neutral key with blue backlight, aspirational and conclusive final frame.
