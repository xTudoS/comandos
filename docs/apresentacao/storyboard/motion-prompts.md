# Motion Prompts — Webinar Comando

Prompts de movimento para gerar os clipes de IA (Runway / Pika / SVD) a partir dos panels do sequence board. Cada prompt foca em movimento, direção, ritmo e duração. Manter o look Apple-like minimal, vidro fosco, fundo off-white, acento azul.

## Motion 1 (Sequence A, Panel 1→2) — caos
A dozen translucent app windows jitter and crowd around a seated professional, one extra notification badge popping in, then the windows slowly begin to drain of color and tilt toward the center. Camera pushes in very slightly. Restless start easing into a calming shift. 4 seconds.

## Motion 2 (Sequence A, Panel 3→4) — convergência
The colorless windows dissolve into soft particles and stream inward, converging into a single frosted-glass panel as the Comando logo resolves crisply at center. Camera holds steady, settling. Smooth decelerating motion, chaos to calm. 4 seconds.

## Motion 3 (Sequence A, Panel 4 hold) — logo respira
The Comando logo rests centered on frosted glass over an off-white field; a gentle blue glow breathes softly behind it and faint particles drift. Camera perfectly still. Slow, serene, premium. 3 seconds.

## Motion 4 (Beat 3) — mapa orbital
Eight simple line icons orbit slowly and smoothly around the central Comando logo, thin blue arcs drawing themselves between each icon and the center one by one. Camera gently rotates a few degrees. Calm, continuous, explanatory pace. 5 seconds.

## Motion 5 (Sequence B) — ponte do tempo real
A task card detaches from the left screen and glides left-to-right along a bright blue thread, landing on the right "Maria" screen where a "→ Maria" tag lights up. Camera tracks the card across the gap. Quick, fluid, satisfying snap at the end. 4 seconds.

## Motion 6 (Beat 5) — offline para sincronizado
A task card is edited while a muted amber offline icon pulses in the corner; then a single clean blue pulse ripples outward across the interface as it reconnects and a checkmark settles on the card. Camera static. Steady, then one decisive sweep. 4 seconds.

## Motion 7 (Sequence C) — encerramento/CTA
On an empty off-white field, a frosted-glass surface fades in, the Comando logo resolves, the tagline fades in below, then a softly glowing "Comece agora" button appears. Camera slowly pushes in a touch. Graceful, building, conclusive. 5 seconds.
